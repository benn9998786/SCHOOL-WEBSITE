A Message from Our Principal

“Education is not merely about acquiring knowledge; it is about discovering one's potential and using it to make a meaningful difference in the world.”

Since 1979, Fr. Agnel Higher Secondary School has helped young people from Pilar and beyond grow in knowledge, character and confidence, guided by our motto, “Always Higher”.
