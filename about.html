Since 1979

Our Story

From two streams in 1979 to a full range of general and vocational courses, the school has served the youth of Pilar and its neighbourhood for over four decades.

June 1979

The Beginning

The late Fr. Philomeno D’Costa sfx founded the school with the Arts and Commerce streams to meet the needs of young people from the neighbourhood.

June 1980

Science Stream Added

A year later, the Science stream opened, adding Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics.

Vocational Era

Skills for Careers

When the Central Government first brought vocational education to Goa, the school started the Vocational stream with four courses: Computer Technique, Auto Engineering Technology, Office Secretaryship & Stenography, and Insurance.

Today

Always Higher

The school is run by the Xaverian Educational Society (Pilar Fathers), registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. It continues to aim “Always Higher”.

Our Patron · Beyond the Ordinary

Ven. Fr. Agnelo de Souza

Fr. Agnelo was born in Anjuna, Goa, on the feast of St. Agnes, and was named Agnel, “little lamb”. He lost both parents as a boy but stayed a brilliant student. He topped his classes, studied philosophy and theology at Rachol Seminary, and taught Latin and Portuguese before joining the Society of Pilar.

He served as a pastor, preacher, confessor and spiritual guide, and was known for living exactly what he preached. He collapsed in the pulpit while preaching on the Vespers of the Sacred Heart and died the next morning. Today he is a model and intercessor for the Pilar family.
